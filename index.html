Licence copy is displayed at our registered office as required. Available for inspection by clients on request.

  • Training. Guards and supervisors complete the PSARA-mandated minimum of 100 classroom + 60 field hours over 20 working days at a recognised institute; ex-servicemen complete the condensed 56-hour course.
  • Verification. Character and antecedent verification through police records before any deployment. Guards aged 18 to below 65 only.
  • Identity. Every guard and supervisor carries an agency-issued photo identity card showing name, number, role and validity.
  • Records. Statutory registers of managers, guards and supervisors are maintained and open to inspection by the Controlling Authority.
  • Uniform. Distinct agency uniform that does not resemble police or armed forces dress.
  • Statutory cover. EPF, ESI, minimum wages and workmen's compensation for all deployed staff.
