1. Security services

Licensed guarding and protection

Security is the agency's core work. We hold a licence from the Controlling Authority, Haryana Police, under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005. Every guard is trained to the statutory standard, verified by the police, and supervised on site.

1.1

Manned guarding

Trained guards and supervisors for offices, housing societies, factories, warehouses, hospitals, and schools. Each site has written standing orders, fixed shift timings, and unannounced checks by a supervisor.

1.2

Armed guards

Armed guards for banks, jewellers, and other high-value sites. Only guards who hold a valid arms licence are posted in an armed role.

1.3

Personal security officers

Close protection for business owners, directors, and visitors. Each assignment starts with a route plan and a survey of the places to be visited.

1.4

Event security

Entry control, frisking, and crowd management for weddings, conferences, concerts, and exhibitions.

1.5

CCTV and electronic surveillance

Design, installation, and maintenance of cameras, access control, and intrusion alarms.

1.6

Facility support

Housekeeping, fire marshals, and reception staff, supplied with guarding under one contract.

4. Licence and compliance

How we meet the law

We operate under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005 and the Haryana Private Security Agencies Rules, 2022. The licence details below are copied from the certificate.

A copy of the licence is displayed at the registered office. Clients may inspect it on request.

  1. Training. Guards and supervisors complete at least 100 hours in class and 60 hours in the field, over 20 working days, at a recognised institute. Ex-servicemen complete a shorter course of 56 hours.
  2. Verification. The police verify each guard's character and antecedents before deployment.
  3. Identity. Each guard and supervisor carries a photo identity card issued by the agency. It shows the holder's name, number, role, and validity date.
  4. Records. We keep the statutory register of managers, supervisors, and guards. The Controlling Authority may inspect it.
  5. Uniform. Our uniform does not resemble police or armed forces dress.
  6. Statutory cover. Every deployed employee is covered by EPF, ESI, minimum wages, and workmen's compensation.
