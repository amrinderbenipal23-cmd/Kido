# Scroll-Driven Compass Hero Wins Security Trust

The best template for this site is not any guarding-company website but the scroll-driven cybersecurity and defense-tech sites recognised on Awwwards (SCS, CyberConvoy, Advenica, Suridata), re-skinned in a deep-navy base with a sparing gold accent and anchored on a single compass motif that literally "finds north" as the visitor scrolls. The "3D" in every 2026 tutorial on this genre is scroll-scrubbed AI video, not WebGL: the production recipe is one continuous ~15-second Higgsfield film (Soul Cinema still → Cinema Studio 3.0 or a DoP 360 Orbit preset, 1080p, audio off, no baked-in text), re-encoded as an all-keyframe H.264 MP4 that scroll position seeks through, with every headline kept as live HTML and a small Three.js r187 compass-and-particle scene layered over it for cursor response. The front-end stack is pinned to three 0.187.0 via import map, GSAP 3.15.1 (free, including ScrollTrigger and SplitText), Lenis 1.3.27, and CSS scroll-driven animations (Baseline since Firefox 159 in October 2026) for reveals, with a reduced-motion ladder that swaps everything for a preloaded poster. Legally, the site must identify the agency as licensed under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005 and the Haryana Private Security Agencies Rules, 2022 by the Controlling Authority (ADGP Law & Order, Haryana Police), publish the licence number, validity and district/State scope exactly as on the certificate, limit training claims to the Model Rules' 160-hour/56-hour regime, never imply NCR or pan-India coverage, keep DGR empanelment distinct from the licence, and avoid police/IAF-like insignia. Deployment is Cloudflare Pages (25 MiB per-file cap, unmetered bandwidth), lead capture is Web3Forms plus a WhatsApp deep link, and SEO rests on JSON-LD `LocalBusiness` + `ProfessionalService` with a 13-item `hasOfferCatalog` and a verified Google Business Profile. Two client-side ambiguities must be resolved before launch: what "PRC at home" means (the only attested Indian expansion is Permanent Residence Certificate) and the actual PSARA certificate data.

## Guarding incumbents look dated; cyber and defense sites set the motion bar

Award-level motion design in the security sector belongs almost entirely to cybersecurity and defense-tech firms. The one physical-security brand with an Awwwards listing is **SCS, a firm operating in 30+ countries, which earned an Honorable Mention in July 2026 for a site built around scroll-driven motion** ([Awwwards: SCS](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/scs)). The other sector entries worth opening first are CyberConvoy ("futuristic", "techie and elegant", with its own scroll-animation inspiration entry), Suridata ("original animations and interactions that tell its brand story"), Advenica (high-assurance national-defense security built around "trust and clear UX") and SEQRYPTON (animated page-load and video elements) ([Awwwards: CyberConvoy](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/cyberconvoy); [Suridata](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/suridata); [Advenica](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/advenica-com); [SEQRYPTON](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/seqrypton)). The guarding incumbents, by contrast, are consistently praised for breadth and faulted for looks: Fireart's roundup calls GardaWorld "comprehensive and professional, with a global outlook" and still recommends "a more modern and visually appealing design", while United Security Services is told it needs "dynamic visuals" ([Fireart: 14 Examples of Inspirational Security Websites](https://fireart.studio/blog/14-examples-of-inspirational-security-websites)). G4S's much-cited web project was a migration consolidating 102 country sites within an existing Sitecore design system, not a visual redesign ([Builtvisible G4S migration](https://builtvisible.com/case-studies/g4s-migration/)). A Haryana agency that applies SCS-grade scroll choreography therefore competes against IndiaMART-listing-grade competitors locally and corporate-template incumbents nationally; the opening is wide.

The palette decision is better evidenced than most design choices. The defense-tech category is criticised as "dull, but seems to be nearly industry wide", tending "toward beige, off-white, and black palettes" so interchangeable that "you could swap either brand [Anduril/Helsing] around with little effort" ([Summary Judgement](https://summaryjudgement.substack.com/p/redux-defencetech-marketing-battle)). The published security-brand pattern that avoids this is navy as the trust base with gold as a rationed prestige accent: Paladin Security's style guide names "Knight's Navy" and "Aegis Gold" as its main colours, with navy conveying "success, security, confidence" and gold "warmth, courage, and honour" ([Paladin Security Style Guide](https://paladinsecurity.com/styleguide)), and the Pacific Northwest Defense Coalition identity uses **gold #BD8825 and slate blue #416174 on a deep navy base**, with the gold "used sparingly to draw focus to the emblem" ([DesignRush: PNDC](https://www.designrush.com/best-designs/logo/pacific-northwest-defense-coalition-logo-design)). Veteran-founded security identities repeat this: TMG2S pairs "CISSP · MBA · USMC Veteran" with a gold reticle mark ([TMG2S About](https://tmg2s.pages.dev/about)). Typography across the sector is a clean geometric or grotesque sans: Anduril uses Helvetica Now ([Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/32662/anduril-industries)), SecretumVault's brand guide pairs Space Grotesk with gold on deep navy ([SecretumVault brand guidelines](https://repo.jesusperez.pro/jesus/secretumvault/blame/branch/main/assets/branding/brand-guidelines.md)). No source supports serif display type here. The working token set is a navy base around #0B1A2F–#0B1A33, gunmetal surfaces, off-white text, one muted gold (#BD8825–#C9A24A) covering under 10% of the surface, and an optional cool blue for links and glow; only #BD8825, #416174 and #ffd700 are sourced hex values, the rest are design inference.

The 2026 trend consensus maps cleanly onto that palette. Dark mode is now a default experience with a light toggle, provided contrast ratios for text and buttons hold ([DesignRush: 17 Web Design Trends for 2026](https://www.designrush.com/agency/website-design-development/trends/web-design-trends)); glassmorphism has become "layered" with multiple depth layers and context-dependent transparency rather than one frosted panel ([Muzli](https://store.muz.li/articles/landing-page-design-trends-2026)); kinetic typography "has moved beyond the experimental stage" but "must remain readable"; custom cursors must degrade because "there is no cursor on touch devices" ([Studio Meyer](https://studiomeyer.io/en/blog/webdesign-trends-2026)); and every source insists that spatial animation be triggered by scroll, hover or click and respect reduced-motion preferences ([Line25: Web Design Trends 2026](https://line25.com/articles/web-design-trends-2026/)). One guide names "Lenis + GSAP ScrollTrigger + Three.js" as the production stack for premium scroll-driven 3D ([Svilenkovic](https://svilenkovic.com/3d/scrollytelling-trends-2026)); treat its "23% more scroll depth" statistic as unsupported, since it cites no study.

### Thirteen services need a two-axis mega menu, not a thirteen-tile wall

The consulting breadth (financial management, cybersecurity, HR, data science, AI/ML, robotics, resume making, agriculture, banking, investment decisions, PRC at home, aptitude tests) is the site's largest design risk, because a guarding brand that looks like a general-services marketplace loses the trust it is trying to build. Professional-services navigation guidance says to **limit top-level navigation to seven items** and put a search box on every page ([Hinge Marketing](https://hingemarketing.com/blog/story/7-website-navigation-tips-to-enhance-the-user-experience)), to organise the Services mega menu in two or three bold-headed columns by intent, for example "Practice Areas | Industries We Serve | Client Resources | Why Choose Us" ([TinyFrog](https://tinyfrog.com/what-is-a-mega-menu/); [Belov Digital](https://belovdigital.agency/blog/how-to-design-effective-mega-menus-for-large-websites/)), to add a hover-open delay or prefer click/tap because hover-triggered mega dropdowns are a known frustrating pattern ([Digital Thrive](https://www.digitalthriveai.com/en-ca/resources/web-design/frustrating-design-patterns-mega-dropdown-hover-menus/); [ZAG Interactive](https://www.zaginteractive.com/insights/articles/june-2025/9-tips-for-good-website-menu-design)), and to mirror the structure as accordions on mobile so desktop menus "feel like enhancements, not completely different experiences" ([Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/designers/ux/multilevel-menu-design)). On the page itself, the bento rule is "each cell = one number, one meaning" ([Contra](https://contra.com/community/nHB2sKks-building-a-digital-presence-that-reflects)). The closest Indian comparator is SIS Group, which runs security, cash logistics and facility management and splits its site into "About Us" and "Corporate & Consumer" sections rather than listing every line at the top ([SIS Corporate & Consumer](https://sisindia.com/corporate-consumer)).

The recommended information architecture follows directly: six top-level items (Services, Sectors, Leadership, Insights, Careers, Contact); a Services mega menu with column A "Security Services" (manned guarding, supervisors, PSOs/executive protection, event security, CCTV and control-room monitoring, security audits), column B "Consulting Practices" (the twelve consulting lines), and column C a featured "Why us / Compliance" panel; and on the homepage two bento blocks, Security and Consulting, with four to six tiles each and a "View all services" link, instead of one thirteen-tile wall. The compass tagline is the device that makes the breadth coherent: every practice is framed as "a direction", and the hero compass needle is the one motion element repeated across section headers.

### Founder credentials convert when they are checkable

The strongest veteran-founder pattern is a named founder with a checkable service record stacked with civilian certifications and externally verifiable status. Diligence Security Group's founder listing reads "Service Branch: U.S. Army; Years Served: 2003–2007; Military Occupation: 96B20 Intelligence Analyst" ([NaVOBA VBE Spotlight](https://www.navoba.org/vbe-spotlight-diligence-security-group)); Shield of Steel says its founder "signs every engagement personally" and cites VA-verified SDVOSB status and a BBB rating ([Shield of Steel About](https://shieldofsteel.com/pages/about)); press coverage treats the veteran origin story itself as the hook ([Military Times](https://www.militarytimes.com/education-transition/2023/05/14/marine-veteran-turns-side-gig-into-security-company/)). In NCR the convention is rank-first: Absolute Security & Allied Services lists a "Major (Retd)" as founder ([Absolute Services About Us](https://absoluteservices.in/?p=2129)). **A Squadron Leader is the IAF equivalent of an Army Major and a Navy Lieutenant Commander** ([PMF IAS](https://www.pmfias.com/equivalent-ranks-defence-services-pyq-2024/)), so the Leadership section should style the founder "Squadron Leader [Name] (Retd), Indian Air Force", add the "equivalent to Major" line, show a short service timeline, and end with a row of badges (PSARA licence number, GST, any DGR empanelment, ISO) that link out to the issuing body. Security sites "win because they reduce doubt" ([One Firefly](https://mail.onefirefly.com/expertise/blog/security-company-website-trust-signals)); the badges, not the particles, do that work.

## Higgsfield: generate one continuous film, scrub it, keep every word in HTML

Higgsfield is a multi-model aggregator with in-house engines, and the relevant subset for this project is narrow. Read directly from the live MCP catalog on 16 November 2026: `soul_cinematic` ("Soul Cinema") produces cinema-grade stills at 1.5k/2k including **21:9**, the right ratio for an ultrawide hero; `cinematic_studio_3_0` is image-to-video at **4–15 s, 480p to 4k**, aspect ratios from 21:9 to 9:16, with start/end-image roles and audio off by default; the DoP preset engine applies a named camera move to a single still; `hf_mult_motion_control` / `hf_mult_replace_object` are Genjutsu (motion transfer and object swap); and the Marketing Studio "Motion" gallery holds 231 brand-motion presets in `saas_motion`, `hypermotion`, `2d_motion` and `mixed_media` families, of which "Signal Red HUD", "Monospace Callouts", "Chrome Core", "Liquid Gold" and "Slow ambient" are the names closest to a control-room aesthetic (an inference from names and previews). Image-to-3D exists too: Meshy, Tripo and Hunyuan models output textured GLB meshes, so a true WebGL compass could be generated from a single still, but no tutorial found actually does this; every "3D website" build in the genre uses scroll-scrubbed video. Vibe Motion, launched 5 February 2026 and "powered by Anthropic's Claude", generates editable motion graphics plus code from prompts ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/02/05/higgsfield-launches-vibe-editor-creating-motion-graphics/)), though one practitioner reports "high render failure rates" ([Greg Preece](https://gregpreece.com/articles/how-i-build-saas-motion-graphics-with-higgsfield-in-under-5-minutes)). Version claims for Cinema Studio 4.0 (native 4K, one-minute takes) conflict with the MCP catalog entry (480p/720p listed) and remain unresolved; build on 3.0.

Higgsfield's own website-builder skill, read in full from the MCP, is the single most useful source because it states the footage contract outright: "One continuous move — no hard cuts… Stage it as a single unbroken camera move (slow orbit, push-in, rise, fly-through)"; "One hero subject, kept centered, with clean negative space"; "A background the copy can survive. Dark, seamless, low-detail (studio black/charcoal, a soft gradient, the subject emerging from darkness)"; "Slow, steady motion — constant speed"; "Locked exposure and white balance, no flicker"; and **"No on-screen text, logos, or watermarks — all type is HTML over the video."** Its default journey is single-shot, "ONE continuous ~15s film, generated in ONE call, scrubbed end to end", produced by first generating one 16:9 storyboard image "laying the film out as 6 keyframes of a single continuous move, in a 6-panel grid… NOT six different scenes", then one `generate_video` call using that storyboard as a style reference (not as `start_image`, or "the page opens on a static storyboard for a second or two"), at the longest take the model supports, highest resolution, audio off, with the prompt naming "the grade and the hexes" and restating "no cuts, no camera shake, slow steady motion only, locked exposure, no on-screen text." The same skill demands one byte-identical "world grammar" preamble (palette, light direction, surface finish, lens) reused across every still and clip so assets match.

For camera presets, Higgsfield advertises "20+ presets, from dolly zooms to FPV drone sweeps" ([Higgsfield blog](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/1WI9XRWB5sIE3N7LfSUjQI)), with 360 Orbit, 3D Rotation, Dolly In/Out, Crane Up/Down, Arc, FPV Drone, Crash Zoom, Bullet Time, Whip Pan and Handheld among the named moves ([clipia.ai DoP listing](https://clipia.ai/en/video-models/higgsfield-dop)), and Director Mode lets you stack up to three moves per shot ([Higgsfield help center](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/tools-and-workflows/how-do-i-use-cinema-studio)). Only one of these is intrinsically loopable: **a full 360 Orbit returns to its start pose**, which makes it the preset for an autoplaying hero loop and the natural fit for the compass (camera revolves while the needle settles north). 3D Rotation (the compass itself turning) is the alternative; Dolly In and Crane Up suit scroll-scrubbed, non-looping films; Crash Zoom, Whip Pan, Handheld and Bullet Time are wrong for a corporate-security tone; FPV Drone belongs in a perimeter fly-through section, not the hero. The asset plan that follows is composed from the cited rules rather than copied from any tested prompt, so budget two re-rolls per asset.

| Asset | Model / preset | Ratio, length | Role on page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compass hero loop | Soul Cinema still → DoP 360 Orbit or Cinema Studio 3.0 | 16:9 or 21:9, 10–15 s, 1080p, audio off | Autoplay loop behind "Find your North" |
| Journey film | Cinema Studio 3.0 single-shot via 6-panel storyboard | 16:9, ~15 s | Scroll-scrubbed storytelling section |
| Control room | Soul Cinema still → Dolly In | 16:9, 8–10 s | Security services section |
| Radar sweep | Marketing Studio `hypermotion` or Cinema Studio 3.0 | 1:1 or 16:9 loop | Compliance / monitoring tile |
| Perimeter night aerial | FPV Drone, slow constant altitude | 16:9, 8–10 s | Sectors section, scrubbed only |
| Officer portraits | Soul 2.0 with one Soul ID for consistency | 3:4 or 4:3 stills | Leadership and Careers |

A reusable preamble reads: "Premium corporate security brand film. Palette: deep navy #0B1A33 to charcoal black #0A0A0C, accents of brushed gold #C9A24A; single cool key light from upper left, soft gold rim light; matte metal and dark glass surfaces; 35mm anamorphic look, shallow depth of field; locked exposure, no flicker, no on-screen text, no logos, no watermarks." The hero prompt appends: "A brass-and-black marine compass rests centered on a dark polished slab, needle slowly settling to north, faint gold etched meridian lines; camera performs one slow, continuous 360-degree orbit at constant speed, returning to the exact starting angle; no cuts." Keep the subject centre-safe with negative space on the side where the HTML headline will sit.

Do not export thousands of frame images." ### Encode, license and budget

Higgsfield delivers H.264 MP4 (its CDN previews are `*-h264-600-v1.mp4` with WebP thumbnails) and does not export WebM natively; its own skill ships the web recipe: desktop at native resolution with `-c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 20 -pix_fmt yuv420p -g 8 -keyint_min 8 -sc_threshold 0 -an -movflags +faststart`, a mobile variant capped at 720p with CRF 23 and GOP 4, the poster extracted as the exact first frame of the encoded clip, a delivery budget of **≤32 MiB for all desktop clips and ≤16 MiB for all mobile clips**, a `blob:` entry in the CSP `media-src` for blob-backed seeking, and raw clips kept out of `public/`. For the autoplaying loop (as opposed to the scrubbed film) add a VP9 WebM variant with `-c:v libvpx-vp9 -crf 35 -b:v 0 -an` and list it first in the `<video>` ([salivity WebM loop guide](https://salivity.github.io/webm/article/how-to-create-a-seamless-webm-loop-with-ffmpeg)); hide any loop seam with a last-to-first-frame crossfade ([skills.sh video-loop](https://skills.sh/coroboros/agent-skills/video-loop)). VP9 is roughly 30–50% smaller than H.264 at equal quality and AV1 another 20–30%, at the cost of 5–10x slower encodes and higher CPU on phones without hardware decode ([PowerCommerce](https://powercommerce.com/blogs/ecommerce-hub/the-best-video-formats-for-the-web-choosing-encoding-and-delivering-videos-that-load-fast-and-look-great)).

On rights, Higgsfield's help center states that the company "does not claim ownership of outputs" (Terms §4.4), takes only a non-exclusive licence to operate the service (§4.3), permits commercial use including client work, forbids using outputs to train AI models, and leaves third-party IP and likeness responsibility with the user ([Higgsfield Help Center](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/account-and-privacy/who-owns-my-generations-and-can-i-use-them-commercially)); pricing round-ups say paid plans include commercial use and the Free plan does not ([creatify](https://creatify.ai/blog/higgsfield-pricing-(2026)-plans-and-what-you-ll-actually-pay)), and the Terms were updated effective 27 August 2026 ([Higgsfield blog](https://higgsfield.ai/es/blog/terms-of-use-privacy-policy-update)). Generate on a paid plan, keep job IDs, and use no real guard likenesses or logos the client does not own. Costs are modest: the live pricing widget on 16 November 2026 showed top-ups at **500 credits for $24.65 and 1,000 for $47.60** (42–48% off list in a pre-Black-Friday sale), auto-refill at 20 credits per dollar and 90-day expiry on top-ups; plan tiers vary by region and source (Starter ~$15–19 for 200–270 credits, Plus ~$47–49 for 1,000–1,200) ([Higgsfield pricing explained](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/higgsfield-pricing-explained); [krea.ai](https://www.krea.ai/blog/higgsfield-pricing-explained-2026-unlimited-credits-and-real-monthly-costs)). A 1080p hero plus two re-rolls and a handful of stills lands in the low tens of dollars.

## PSARA and the Haryana 2022 Rules dictate the footer, not the hero

The governing law is the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005 (Act 29 of 2005), whose official text, updated to 15 May 2026, is on India Code ([India Code A2005-29.pdf](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2024/1/A2005-29.pdf)). Haryana implements it through the Haryana Private Security Agencies Rules, 2022, approved by Cabinet on **5 April 2022** in supersession of the 2009 Rules and framed on the Centre's Private Security Agencies Central Model Rules, 2020 ([Haryana CM office release](https://mail.manoharlalkhattar.in/node/25772)), which MHA notified as G.S.R. 776(E) on 15 December 2020 ([StaffNews notification](https://www.staffnews.in/?p=110770)). The Controlling Authority is the Additional Director General of Police (Law and Order), Haryana Police, at Police Headquarters, Sector 6, Panchkula ([Haryana PSA Rules 2009](https://boardhr.blogspot.com/2020/05/haryana-private-security-agencies-rules.html); [Compliance Calendar, Haryana](https://www.compliancecalendar.in/psara-license-in-haryana)). Licensing went fully online on 1 November 2020 through the Haryana Police website ([ANI, 30 Oct 2020](https://aniweb.voxturr.us/news/national/general-news/private-security-agency-licensing-time-goes-online-in-haryana-from-november-120201030232835)) and is now integrated into the national MHA portal ([psara.gov.in](https://psara.gov.in/)); Haryana had licensed over 1,000 agencies by July 2020 ([SecurityToday](https://securitytoday.in/licenses-granted-in-haryana-to-over-1000-private-security-agencies)), which is the competitive field. Three statutory facts shape what the site may say: a licence is valid **five years** and renewable for five more under s.7(5) ([Indian Kanoon, s.7](https://future.indiankanoon.org/doc/1346920/)); renewal must be filed **not less than 45 days before expiry** under s.8(1); and under s.7(3) the fee, and therefore the licence itself, is tiered by whether the agency operates in one district, two to five districts, or the whole State, reported for Haryana at Rs 5,000, Rs 10,000 and Rs 25,000 ([Compliance Calendar fees](https://www.compliancecalendar.in/learn/psara-license-fees-india)). Because scope is an attribute of the licence, **a Haryana licence is not valid in Delhi or UP** ([Compliance Calendar, district-wise vs state-wise](https://www.compliancecalendar.in/learn/district-wise-vs-state-wise-psara-license)), so any "NCR" or "pan-India" claim is false unless separate licences exist.

The Act regulates premises, uniforms, ID cards and records rather than websites, and no provision specifically governing advertising or websites was found; the website obligations below are therefore credibility practice derived from statutory duties, not a line-item mandate. The Model Rules 2020 require the physical licence to be delivered to the registered office within 15 days, "which the private security agency shall be bound to display at its place of business" ([Legitquest, Model Rules 2020](https://www.legitquest.com/act/private-security-agencies-central-model-rules-2020/9D5E)); s.15 requires a register of managers, guards and supervisors, s.16 provides for inspection, and s.17 requires a photo identity card for every guard and supervisor ([Lawgist Act index](https://lawgist.in/private-security-agencies-regulation-act)); s.20(2) sets a fine of up to Rs 25,000 plus suspension or cancellation for contravening ss.9, 10 and 12 ([Legal Authority bare act](https://www.legalauthority.in/bare-act/private-security-agencies-regulation-act-2005)); and MHA has recorded concern in Parliament about guards wearing uniforms resembling the police ([Rajya Sabha reply, Feb 2021](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/714318/1/PQ_253_10022021_U990_p163_p165.pdf)). That last point reaches straight into the Higgsfield prompts: no khaki, camouflage, service insignia, IAF roundel or government emblem in any generated frame, and no "Government approved" copy, since a licence is a permission, not an endorsement.

| Licence line | "Licensed under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005 and the Haryana Private Security Agencies Rules, 2022 by the Controlling Authority (ADGP Law & Order), Haryana Police. Licence No. [PSARA/HR/XXXX], valid [from]–[to], covering [districts / whole State of Haryana]" | ss.4, 7, 8; placeholders until the certificate is in hand |
| Training statement | "Every guard completes PSARA-prescribed training (minimum 160 hours over 20 working days at a Controlling-Authority-recognised institute; condensed 56-hour course for ex-servicemen), police/antecedent verification, and carries an agency photo ID" | Model Rules r.8; s.17 |
| Eligibility (Careers page) | Age 18 to under 65; ex-servicemen welcome | s.10(1) |
| Other registrations | GST, EPF/ESI, CIN as applicable | Trust signal, not PSARA |
| Grievance route | Named officer, phone, SLA | Good practice; no statutory mandate found |

The training numbers are the most quotable compliance content on the site and must be stated exactly. Under Rule 8 of the Model Rules 2020 guards and supervisors complete **at least 100 hours of classroom and 60 hours of field training over at least 20 working days**, while ex-servicemen and former police take a condensed **40 + 16 hours over at least 7 working days**; the licensee must complete 6 working days of training, and training institutes are recognised for five-year terms ([MHA SOP for training institutes, 4 May 2023](https://xn--i1b5bzbybhfo5c8b4bxh.xn--11b7cb3a6a.xn--h2brj9c/sites/default/files/Sop_04052023%20.pdf); [abcaus Model Rules highlights](https://abcaus.in/govt/private-security-agencies-central-model-rules-2020-key-highlights.html)). Section 10(1) bars engaging anyone under 18 or who has attained 65 ([Indian Kanoon, s.10](https://future.indiankanoon.org/doc/1595530)). The police verify antecedents; they do not certify guards, so "Haryana Police certified" is a false claim. Cash-in-transit is a separately regulated activity under the Haryana Private Security Agencies (Private Security to Cash Transportation Activities) Rules, 2019, requiring GPS-equipped vans, a driver plus two armed guards and two custodians, a Rs 5 crore per-trip cap, and vaults with 90-day CCTV retention ([Indian Kanoon, Haryana 2019 Rules](https://future.indiankanoon.org/doc/23195993/)); do not list it, or armed guards generally, unless arms licences and that compliance exist.

The founder's service background creates one specific trap. The Directorate General Resettlement under the Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare "empanels/sponsors ESM-run Private Security Agencies" to supply guards to CPSEs and corporates ([DESW Security Agency Scheme](https://www.desw.gov.in/Self-Employment-Schemes-DGR/security-agency-scheme)); registration is restricted to ex-servicemen officers, made only after retirement, and limited to one State per officer ([PIB release, MoD](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2040794)); in May 2025 DGR issued **46 empanelment certificates and sponsored 3,166 ESM guards** ([DESW Veterans Welfare Newsletter, June 2025](https://www.desw.gov.in/sites/default/files/veterans_welfare_newsletter_June2025.pdf)); and courts describe it as "only a measure to enable the ex-servicemen to adapt and establish themselves to a civilian life" with a quota of guards per officer ([Delhi High Court, Apr 2026](https://delhihighcourt.nic.in/app/showFileJudgment/VKR09042026LPA7052023_115904.pdf)). Empanelment is a sponsorship, not a licence: the site may say "DGR-empanelled for the State of Haryana, Certificate No. [placeholder]" only if the founder holds a current certificate, and must never present it as equivalent to, or a substitute for, the PSARA licence. Defensible language is "veteran-led", "ex-servicemen supervisors" and "trained to armed-forces discipline"; prohibited language is "Ministry of Defence approved" or "Government of India security agency". Three items remain unverified and need checking against primary documents before copy is finalised: the gazette notification number and date of the Haryana 2022 Rules (only the Cabinet approval was found), the verbatim text of s.12 (licence exhibition) and s.22 (uniform offences), and any 2024–26 Haryana change to fees or forms. No source documents a standard Haryana licence-number format, so the placeholder must stay visibly a placeholder until the certificate arrives, and the Controlling Authority should be named by post, never by the officer's name, because the only name found dates from 2020.

## Pin three r187, GSAP 3.15.1 and Lenis 1.3.27, and let CSS do the reveals

Library versions were read from the npm registry and the published tarballs on 16 November 2026, which are byte-identical to what jsDelivr and unpkg serve. **three is 0.187.0 (r187, published 30 October 2026)** and ships no `three.min.js` at all: `build/` holds only `three.module.js` (659 KB raw / 129 KB gzip), `three.core.js` (1.47 MB / 290 KB gzip) and the WebGPU/TSL builds, with the module file importing `./three.core.js` relatively, so both download ([npm registry: three](https://registry.npmjs.org/three); [tarball](https://registry.npmjs.org/three/-/three-0.187.0.tgz)). Addons such as `examples/jsm/postprocessing/UnrealBloomPass.js` import the bare specifier `three`, which makes an import map mandatory; the three.js manual's no-build pattern maps `three` to `cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@<version>/build/three.module.js` and `three/addons/` to `.../examples/jsm/`, warning that every dependency must come from the same version and CDN ([three.js manual](https://threejs.org/manual/en/installation.html); [three.js Discourse example](https://discourse.threejs.org/t/npm-free-threejs/50097/2)). Import maps have been Baseline since Safari 16.4 in March 2023 ([web.dev](https://web.dev/blog/import-maps-in-all-modern-browsers)). jsDelivr will auto-minify when `.min` is appended to a path that lacks one, but its generated files carry a header warning against SRI ([jsDelivr readme](https://github.com/jsdelivr/jsdelivr)). **GSAP is 3.15.1 (4 November 2026) with a 4.0.0-beta.4 under the beta tag**; since Webflow's acquisition "GSAP is now 100% FREE including ALL of the bonus plugins like SplitText… even for commercial use" ([gsap README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/gsap@3.15.0/README.md); [Webflow blog](https://webflow.com/blog/gsap-becomes-free)), and `dist/` ships `gsap.min.js` (28.4 KB gzip), `ScrollTrigger.min.js` (18.0 KB), `SplitText.min.js` (3.6 KB) and `ScrollSmoother.min.js` (5.5 KB) ([gsap tarball](https://registry.npmjs.org/gsap/-/gsap-3.15.1.tgz)); stay on 3.x in production because the 4.x beta description already signals API changes. **Lenis is 1.3.27 (15 October 2026)** at 5.4 KB gzip with 2.0 still `-dev` ([npm registry: lenis](https://registry.npmjs.org/lenis)); its README gives the ScrollTrigger glue (`lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update); gsap.ticker.add(t => lenis.raf(t * 1000)); gsap.ticker.lagSmoothing(0)`), honours reduced motion by default (`respectReducedMotion: true` forces `lerp` to 1), and documents its limits: no CSS scroll-snap, 60 fps cap on Safari, 30 fps in low-power mode ([lenis README](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis)).

Two incompatibilities matter. Vanta's README loads `three.min.js` r134 as a global before its effects and says "Make sure three.js or p5.js has already been included via <script> tag" ([npm registry: vanta](https://registry.npmjs.org/vanta)); r187 has no global build, so using Vanta means shipping two copies of three.js. Choose a hand-written r187 scene for the compass hero and drop Vanta, or use Vanta alone with its own pinned r134 and no custom scene. Spline's hosted `<spline-viewer>` bundles both rendering engines at 3.6 MB raw / 1.0 MB gzip ([viewer tarball](https://registry.npmjs.org/@splinetool/viewer/-/viewer-2.0.184.tgz)); if Spline is used at all, import the 38 KB `runtime.js` entry, self-host the `.splinecode` to avoid CORS, and reserve it for a single showcase object. The Spline runtime has no license field in the registry, so check docs.spline.design for commercial terms.

### The hero: video underneath, WebGL compass on top, poster as LCP

The hero is a z-stack: the Higgsfield loop at `z-index: 0` with `object-fit: cover` and a `saturate(.8) brightness(.55)` filter, a transparent WebGL `<canvas>` at `z-index: 1` with `pointer-events: none` and `mix-blend-mode: screen` so wireframe and particles glow additively over the footage, a radial vignette pseudo-element for legibility, and the HTML copy on top. The `<video>` carries `autoplay muted loop playsinline preload="metadata"` (iOS requires both `muted` and `playsinline`) and a first-frame AVIF/WebP `poster`, which web.dev confirms is an LCP candidate and which should be preloaded with `<link rel="preload" as="image" fetchpriority="high">` ([web.dev video performance](https://web.dev/learn/performance/video-performance)). That one decision keeps LCP on a ~60 KB image rather than a multi-megabyte video or a canvas. The Three.js scene itself is small: a `Group` holding a wireframe `SphereGeometry(1.6, 32, 24)` globe, a thin `TorusGeometry` compass ring rotated flat, a `ConeGeometry` needle pointing +Y as "north", and a `Points` cloud of ~2,500 vertices scattered in a shell; pointer position is normalised to −1…1 and lerped at 0.05 per frame into group rotation, a ScrollTrigger with `scrub: true` on the hero writes a 0–1 progress that adds rotation and pulls the camera back, `setPixelRatio(Math.min(devicePixelRatio, 2))` caps fill cost, a `ResizeObserver` handles layout, and an `IntersectionObserver` plus `visibilitychange` cancel the `requestAnimationFrame` loop whenever the canvas is off-screen or the tab hidden, the exact fix three.js forum users needed when render loops kept running off-screen ([three.js Discourse](https://discourse.threejs.org/t/three-js-gsap-scrolltrigger-video-performance-issue-on-low-end-devices/78051)). The fallback ladder is: WebGL2/WebGL available and no reduced-motion preference → scene; otherwise remove the canvas and unhide a static poster `<img>`; JavaScript disabled → the `<video poster>` and `<img>` still paint. Swap the reference snippet's teal and amber (`0x2dd4bf`, `0xf59e0b`) for the brand gold and a muted blue if the navy/gold palette is adopted; the Codrops interactive-particles technique ([Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2019/01/17/interactive-particles-with-three-js/)) is the upgrade path if the particles must displace near the pointer rather than merely rotate. Optional bloom via `EffectComposer`, `RenderPass` and `UnrealBloomPass` (all present in r187's `examples/jsm/postprocessing/`) should be skipped on mobile because post-processing doubles fill-rate cost. No single live demo combining globe, compass and cursor-reactive particles was found; the pattern is synthesised from the public API and must be tested on a mid-range Android.

### CSS scroll-driven animations carry the reveals; GSAP carries the pins

Scroll-driven animations became **Baseline Newly available in October 2026 when Firefox 159 enabled them by default**, joining Chrome/Edge 115 (July 2023) and Safari 26 (September 2025, with threaded compositor timelines in 26.4) ([web.dev: New to the web platform in October 2026](https://web.dev/blog/web-platform-10-2026); [WebKit guide](https://www.webkit.org/blog/17101/a-guide-to-scroll-driven-animations-with-just-css/)). Some blogs still claim Firefox shipped earlier or remains behind a flag; web.dev's dated timeline is authoritative. Because a meaningful share of Firefox < 159 and Safari < 26 users remain, wrap usage in `@supports (animation-timeline: view())` and provide a ScrollTrigger fallback only where `CSS.supports('animation-timeline: view()')` is false ([BuildMVPFast](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/blog/css-scroll-driven-animations-replace-js-2026)). Use `animation-timeline: view()` with `animation-range: entry 10% entry 60%` for card and bento reveals, and `animation-timeline: scroll(root)` for page-level progress, which is where the compass pays off a second time: a header compass needle whose rotation is bound to document scroll sweeps toward north as the reader reaches the contact section, with zero JavaScript. Reserve GSAP ScrollTrigger for what CSS cannot express, pinned storytelling sections and the scrubbed-video timeline, and keep to one pinned sequence per page; GSAP's own guidance is to pin only necessary elements, debounce `ScrollTrigger.refresh()`, pause off-screen animations and prefer transform and opacity ([GSAP performance skill](https://www.skills.sh/greensock/gsap-skills/gsap-performance)). One caution: Lenis smooths the real scroll position, so heavy CSS scroll-timelines lag the wheel under it; if CSS SDA does most of the work, drop Lenis or use GSAP's free ScrollSmoother, which is built to coexist with ScrollTrigger. Higgsfield's template adds the rule to "never attach a second scrub timeline to the same video elements."

The remaining micro-interactions are small and gated. Text reveals use `SplitText.create('[data-split]', { type: 'words,chars', mask: 'words' })` after `document.fonts.ready`, or Splitting.js's CSS variables (`--char-index`) for a pure-CSS stagger ([splitting.js.org guide](https://splitting.js.org/guide.html)); the hero `<h1>` stays real HTML so the crawlable "Find your North" survives the animation. Magnetic buttons follow Codrops' MIT reference ([codrops/MagneticButtons](https://github.com/codrops/MagneticButtons)) in ~25 lines of GSAP, gated with `matchMedia('(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)')` so touch devices skip them; 3D card tilt needs only `perspective` and `transform-style: preserve-3d` with CSS variables for rotation and a radial-gradient glare ([Motion.dev tilt tutorial](https://motion.dev/tutorials/js-tilt-card)). The bento grid is mobile-first CSS Grid, two columns on phones, four at 768 px, six at 1200 px, with Security and Cybersecurity as 2×2 features, Financial management, AI/ML and Aptitude tests as 2×1 wide tiles (the aptitude tile hosting the interactive demo), and the remaining eight lines as 1×1.

### Budgets, reduced motion and mobile

Core Web Vitals "good" thresholds at the 75th percentile are **LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1** ([web.dev: defining CWV thresholds](https://web.dev/articles/defining-core-web-vitals-thresholds)), and animation that is "buttery on a desktop GPU can blow the 200ms INP budget on a real phone" ([Core Web Vitals checklist 2026](https://www.corewebvitals.io/core-web-vitals/ultimate-checklist)). The LCP element must "render in its final position on first paint" with any motion afterwards limited to transform and opacity ([Hon Tran](https://www.hontran.dev/blog/core-web-vitals-for-animation-heavy-sites)). The proposed budgets for this site are: poster ≤ 60 KB AVIF, preloaded, no entrance animation; hero loop ≤ 4 MB WebM plus ≤ 6 MB MP4 fallback at 1080p, 24–30 fps, 8–12 s, audio stripped, which sits inside every practitioner range found (≤ 5 MB at 5–15 s; stricter guides say < 1.5 MB H.264 for a background layer) ([Adventist Connect hero video guidance](https://support.adventistconnect.org/help/hero-video-best-practices); [BlazingCDN](https://blog.blazingcdn.com/en-us/mp4-vs-webm-best-video-format-for-web-playback)); first-interaction JS ≤ 300 KB gzip, with Three.js loaded as a deferred `type="module"` and additionally gated on idle or first scroll; CLS held near 0.05 by reserving the hero at `min-height: 100svh` with absolutely positioned media; Lottie assets ≤ 100 KB each as dotLottie; total page weight ≤ 8 MB with video, ≤ 1.5 MB without. These megabyte figures are practitioner consensus, not standards; no official Google budget exists for hero video.

Reduced motion is a single switch, not a scattering of checks. One `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')` listener (also honouring `navigator.connection.saveData`) pauses the video and removes `autoplay` so the poster shows, skips Three.js entirely, and routes GSAP through `gsap.matchMedia()` so tweens are scoped to `no-preference` and final states are set directly under `reduce` ([gsap.matchMedia()](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/gsap.matchMedia())). Under reduced motion the video should be swapped for the poster, "no debate" ([Pope Tech](https://blog.pope.tech/?p=11787)). Crucially, **reduced-motion support does not satisfy WCAG 2.2.2**: a looping hero still needs a visible, keyboard-reachable pause control ([hidde.blog: 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide](https://hidde.blog/meeting-2-22-pause-stop-hide-with-prefers-reduced-motion/)). Mobile fallbacks follow Higgsfield's own template, which already handles "desktop/mobile sources, exact-frame posters… iOS gesture priming, prefers-reduced-motion, reverse scroll": serve the 720p GOP-4 variant via `<source media="(max-width: 768px)">`, halve the particle count, cap pixel ratio at 1.5, disable bloom and magnetic hover, use `100svh`, and on `saveData` replace the canvas with the poster; several sources argue that on slow devices "completely disabling background videos and showing only the poster image may provide the best user experience" ([ProfileTree](https://profiletree.com/background-videos/)). Test on a mid-range Android, because that is where INP fails.

## Ship on Cloudflare Pages with Web3Forms and LocalBusiness JSON-LD

Hosting is a one-line decision once the terms are read. **Cloudflare Pages caps a single asset at 25 MiB and a site at 20,000 files** ([Cloudflare Pages limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits)), with unmetered static bandwidth and 500 builds a month ([dev.to comparison](https://dev.to/build996/vercel-vs-netlify-vs-cloudflare-pages-free-frontend-hosting-compared-3h9g)); a 4–6 MB hero loop is far inside the cap. GitHub Pages is ruled out for production because its terms forbid use "as free hosting for online businesses, e-commerce sites, and any other website that is primarily directed at either facilitating commercial transactions or providing commercial software as a service", with a 1 GB site cap and 100 GB-month soft bandwidth ([GitHub Docs: Pages limits](https://docs.github.com/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits)); it remains fine as a staging preview. Vercel Hobby is non-commercial only and Netlify's free tier is now credit-based (~300 credits/month) and pauses sites on exhaustion ([dev.to 2026 comparison](https://dev.to/_6638a39c349d7e9c85ee20/vercel-vs-netlify-vs-cloudflare-pages-2026-best-hosting-for-developers-22ma)). Connect the GitHub repository with no build command and `/` as output, put the `.in` domain on Cloudflare DNS, set `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` on `/media/*` and `/assets/*` through a `_headers` file, add `_redirects` for the thanks page, and use Cloudflare Web Analytics (free, cookieless). Because libraries come from public CDNs and video is small, the repository stays tiny.

Lead capture on a static host means a form backend. **Web3Forms allows ~250 submissions a month free** with unlimited forms and domains, hCaptcha and custom redirects ([splitforms comparison](https://splitforms.com/blog/best-free-form-backend-services-2026)); Formspree's free tier is ~50 a month ([forminit comparison](https://forminit.com/blog/best-form-backend-services-2026/)); Netlify Forms only work on Netlify hosting and their allowance is reported inconsistently. The quote form posts to `https://api.web3forms.com/submit` with a hidden `access_key`, a hidden `botcheck` honeypot, a `redirect`, a required `tel` field with `inputmode="tel"`, a `<select>` of the thirteen services, and a `fetch` enhancement that writes to an `aria-live` status region while remaining functional without JavaScript; a `https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX` WhatsApp deep link is the secondary CTA that Indian SMB leads expect. The aptitude demo is pure client-side JavaScript reading ten questions from `/data/aptitude-sample.json`, with a 180-second timer, scoring, a result card that pre-selects "Aptitude tests" in the quote form, and `localStorage` resume; label it a sample, "not a certified psychometric instrument", to avoid over-claiming.

Local SEO is where the thirteen services and the PSARA footer meet. Google supports JSON-LD, Microdata and RDFa and recommends JSON-LD ([Infidigit](https://www.infidigit.com/?p=177730)); standardise name, address, phone and hours to match the Google Business Profile exactly before writing schema ([Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/?p=3021)). schema.org defines `LocalBusiness` as "a particular physical business or branch of an organization" and `ProfessionalService` as a confirmed subtype ([schema.org/LocalBusiness](https://schema.org/LocalBusiness)); **the existence of a `SecurityService` type could not be confirmed**, so use `"@type": ["LocalBusiness", "ProfessionalService"]` with `serviceType` strings and validate in Google's Rich Results Test. The block carries `telephone`, `priceRange`, a full `PostalAddress` with `addressCountry: "IN"`, `geo`, `areaServed` listing the licensed districts (which keeps schema consistent with the compliance page), `openingHoursSpecification`, `sameAs` to the Maps place and LinkedIn, `slogan: "Find your North"`, and a `hasOfferCatalog` whose thirteen `Offer` items each wrap a `Service`, with the security item named "Security guard & agency services (PSARA-licensed)". Add a `FAQPage` for licensing and pricing questions and `AggregateRating` only if real reviews appear on-page. On the profile side, postcard verification was largely phased out in 2025 and **video verification is now the default for most new listings**: one continuous unedited take in the Google Maps app showing exterior, interior and proof of management, with reviews typically taking 3–5 business days and tightened checks in India against virtual offices and call-centre numbers ([Wiremo GBP video verification checklist](https://wiremo.co/blog/google-business-profile-video-verification/); [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/learn/verifying-google-business-profile/)); set the primary category to "Security guard service" with consulting lines as secondary categories, and keep NAP identical across the site, GBP, JustDial, IndiaMART and Sulekha. Page basics are one `<h1>`, a `<title>` under 60 characters with city and primary service, canonical links, 1200×630 Open Graph images, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `lang="en-IN"`, and `<noscript>` content for the services grid.

One service name needs a decision before it is published anywhere. No web source uses the phrase "PRC at home" as a service; the only widely attested Indian expansion of PRC is **Permanent Residence Certificate**, a state-issued domicile document "particularly important in states with domicile-based reservations in India like Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, and Manipur" and in J&K ([Vedantu](https://www.vedantu.com/general-knowledge/permanent-residency-certificate); [Vikaspedia, Assam](https://vikaspedia.in/aspirational-districts/assam/dhubri/citizen-services/apply-for-prc-permanent-residence-certificate)), so "PRC at home" most plausibly means doorstep help with PRC applications. Pre-Recruitment Counselling, Psychological Readiness Check and recruitment coaching are unsourced alternatives. Spell the expansion out on first use, in the `<title>` and in the schema `Service.name`, and confirm the meaning with the client; a Haryana-based agency offering a North-East domicile service is unusual enough that the copy must explain it.

## Conclusion

The research reframes the brief in three ways. First, "3D motion website" in this genre is a video problem, not a graphics-programming problem: the entire 2026 creator ecosystem around Higgsfield, including Higgsfield's own template, produces scroll-scrubbed cinematic footage with HTML overlaid, and the WebGL layer is a thin, optional garnish; budgeting effort accordingly (most hours into the storyboard, world-grammar preamble and encode pipeline, few into shaders) is the single biggest schedule decision. Second, the compass is not just a tagline illustration but the site's unifying mechanic, able to appear as a 360-orbit hero loop, a scroll-bound CSS needle in the header, and the framing device that turns thirteen unrelated services into "directions" without diluting the guarding brand. Third, in a Haryana market of over a thousand licensed agencies whose web presence is mostly directory listings, the compliance page is a differentiator as potent as the motion: a footer that cites the Act and the 2022 Rules, names the Controlling Authority by post, publishes the licence scope honestly and states the 160-hour training regime signals exactly the "reduce doubt" credibility that security buyers reward.

Going forward, four verifications gate launch rather than design: the PSARA certificate's number, dates and district scope (and any DGR certificate) to replace placeholders; the Haryana 2022 Rules' gazette number and the verbatim s.12/s.22 text before quoting them; the client's intended meaning of "PRC at home"; and a device test of the hero on a mid-range Android, because every performance failure predicted by the sources (INP over 200 ms, LCP on the video instead of the poster, off-screen render loops) shows up there first. Pin the stack now (three 0.187.0, GSAP 3.15.1, Lenis 1.3.27), revisit it only when GSAP 4 and Lenis 2 leave beta, and treat Higgsfield model names as a moving target to be re-read from the MCP catalog on the day of generation.
